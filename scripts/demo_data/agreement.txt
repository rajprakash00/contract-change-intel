MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Services
The Provider shall deliver the managed migration services described in
Statement of Work No. 1.

2. Payment
The Client shall pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt. Late payments
accrue interest at 1% per month.

3. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 14 days written notice.

4. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other party's confidential information for
3 years after termination.

5. Liability
The Provider's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the
preceding 12 months.
